## Information we process

Browsing the public directory does not require registration. Accounts and repository management involve your username, email and verification status, password hash, language preference, and any name, avatar and email visibility choice you provide. Account passwords are not stored as plaintext. External sign-in or linking supplies platform identifiers, usernames and profiles. A platform profile may fill a missing name or avatar.

When you connect GitHub, Gitee or GitLab.com, the Site stores platform identifiers, encrypted access tokens, and any refresh token and expiry supplied by the platform. These support identity checks, direct ownership verification of public repositories, and updates. License files are fetched from public Git addresses; authorization tokens are not used to clone private repositories. You can browse without connecting a platform.

Submissions involve repository names, descriptions, links, platform and owner identifiers, default branches, commit identifiers and dates, Star counts, Fork and archive status, upstream information, license texts and audit evidence. Inclusion status, changes and ranking observations are also stored. Registration records your chosen policy language, all three document versions and the time of consent. Necessary sign-in, security, configuration and operation records support service delivery, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

## Public information

Public pages display usernames, names or display names, avatars, eligible repository details, license labels and rankings. Email is hidden by default and appears on your public profile only if you enable that option. Do not put passwords, tokens, unauthorized personal data or other secrets in public fields.

Removing a submission, disconnecting its platform or deleting your account withdraws the relevant submissions from the public directory and rankings. Third parties may already have saved or indexed information that was public; the Site cannot directly erase their copies.

## Cookies, security checks and external services

The Site uses cookies for sessions, form protection and language preferences. Disabling them may prevent sign-in or form submission. The current application does not integrate advertising trackers or commercial profiling services.

Registration and sign-in use Cloudflare Turnstile. Your browser connects to Cloudflare, which may process IP addresses and browser or device signals for security verification. The Site submits challenge responses for verification. See the [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

Verification messages use the configured email service. Avatars use configured object storage when enabled, and enabled backups use separate storage. GitHub, Gitee and GitLab.com process authorizations and platform data under their own policies. Locations may differ. The operator must supplement this policy with actual storage locations, providers and any required cross-border processing notices. This overview does not replace separate notice or consent where required.

## Retention and deletion

Account and connection information is retained as needed for the corresponding services. Disconnecting deletes the Site's connection and authorization records; you can also revoke the application at the platform. Account deletion deletes the account and related connections, consent records and submissions, removes account links from general audit records, and queues avatar deletion. Storage failures can delay object removal.

Removing a submission does not erase every internal record. Repository metadata, audit evidence and sealed statistics may remain for audit and ranking consistency. Sealed statistics may retain repository snapshots no longer linked to an account after deletion. Log and backup retention depends on deployment. The default backup configuration provides at least a 30-day recovery window; older full backups supporting it may remain longer. A deletion request does not immediately erase every backup copy. The operator must arrange cleanup and post-restoration handling according to necessity and applicable law.

## Your choices and rights

You can correct profile data, disable public email display, disconnect platforms, remove submissions and use the account deletion page. Withdrawing optional information or authorization can affect related features without invalidating earlier lawful processing.

For access, copies, correction or deletion of other personal information, or objections to processing, use the contact email above. “Contact email not set” means that the operator has not supplied this public channel. Existing profile, disconnect, removal and deletion controls remain available. The operator should supply an effective identity and contact channel for requests that cannot be completed through those controls. Do not post identity documents, passwords or tokens in public repository Issues.
